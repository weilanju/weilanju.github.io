Lanju Wei — Interactive Research Portfolio

When market participants hold different information and are connected in different ways, how does that information enter their decisions, and what does the aggregate look like?

Interactive

  • Asset Universe — Stratified Information & Sphere

    33 AI-infrastructure firms, 948 trading days, three layers (price, value, information). Layered and spherical views of how public and channel information move through a supply-chain graph.

  • WTI Stable — Dashboard

    Master's thesis companion: a neural heterogeneous-agent model of WTI, 986 weekly observations.

  • Knowledge Structure Beyond the Transcript

    A map of what I studied outside my formal curricula — the reading that took me from public administration to economics.

Code & Documents

  • market-sim/ — four-agent price-fitting experiment

    Pre-registered protocol, four agent types with different information access, three blind baselines, and the result table — including the null. Reported as it came out.

  • empirical/ — supply-chain information diffusion

    Event study and lead-lag panel on the same 40 firms: do economically linked firms react to each other's disclosures, in what order, and does it survive controls and network nulls.

  • docs/ — design handbook (v1.2.1)

    The working design document for the whole system, including the module that is specified but deliberately not run: the order book and matching engine.

No order book and no matching engine is included. The realized price is exogenous; an order-layer module is specified but not implemented, and no claim here depends on it.
